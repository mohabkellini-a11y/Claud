What We Do

Fire protection engineering, end to end

From sprinkler and alarm design through performance-based modeling, we produce sealed, permit-ready deliverables for MEP firms, architects, contractors, and building owners.

NFPA 13

Fire Sprinkler System Design

Wet, dry, pre-action, and antifreeze system design and layout. Hydraulic calculations, water supply analysis, and PE-stamped shop drawings for delegated design and permit submittal.

NFPA 72

Fire Alarm & Detection Design

Complete fire alarm system design including device layout, riser diagrams, battery and voltage-drop calculations, sequence of operations, and sealed submittal packages.

PE SEAL

Stamping & Third-Party Plan Review

Independent review and PE sealing of sprinkler and fire alarm drawings for contractors and MEP firms. Fast-turnaround delegated design sign-off, comment resolution, and resubmittal support.

FBC / NFPA 101

Code Consulting & Risk Analysis

Life safety and egress analysis, fire risk analysis reports, code compliance studies, and alternative means & methods (AMM) / variance support with your Authority Having Jurisdiction.

FDS / CFD

Fire & Smoke Modeling

Performance-based design using Fire Dynamics Simulator (FDS): smoke control analysis, tenability studies, atrium and warehouse modeling, and egress simulation to support approvals.

SPECIALTY

Mission-Critical & Special Hazards

Data centers and mission-critical facilities, energy storage systems per NFPA 855, clean agent suppression per NFPA 2001, and commissioning support for complex or high-value occupancies.
