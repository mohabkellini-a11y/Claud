Why It Matters

Why PE-stamped matters

Many contractors prepare quality drawings in-house but cannot legally seal them — only a Florida-licensed professional engineer can. Third-party PE stamping gives your submittal the engineering certification the AHJ requires, without the cost of a full engineering department. Our review process catches code issues before the AHJ does, so permits move faster.
