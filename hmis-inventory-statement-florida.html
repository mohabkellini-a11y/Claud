Hazardous Materials Inventory Statements (HMIS) in Florida

When your project stores or uses hazardous materials, the AHJ asks for an inventory statement. EverSafe prepares HMIS documentation listing material classifications, quantities, and storage and use locations per the Florida Building Code and IFC Chapter 50.

What's Included

What's included

  • Hazardous material classification per IFC
  • Quantity tabulation by storage and use, per control area
  • Storage vs. in-use quantities and conditions of use
  • Location plans tied to your floor layouts
  • Coordination with MAQ analysis and hazmat permit applications
Why It Matters

Why the inventory statement matters

The HMIS is the AHJ’s first read on your hazmat risk. A complete, engineer-prepared statement shortens hazmat permit review and gives your MAQ analysis and permit application a defensible technical basis.
